⚠ The civils markup trap — your gross margin can be misleading
When civils / specialist subcontractors are used, their price already includes their profit margin. MES then marks up on top. A 25% gross margin on a £400k ICP job may only represent £15–20k of genuine MES profit after accounting for the sub's embedded ~15% margin on £280k of civils.
What to watch for
› Materials >65% of contract = civils-heavy, check sub markup
› Gross margin 18–22% on large ICP = competitive but tight
› Below 18% on large jobs = rarely profitable after risk
