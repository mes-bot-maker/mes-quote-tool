⚠ The civils markup trap — gross margin overstates your real return
On a £400k ICP job at 25% gross margin, if £280k is civils subcontract with the sub's ~15% already baked in, MES is managing £42k of risk on £280k of civils for maybe £10–15k net. The headline margin looks fine — the actual return on MES risk and effort is thin.
Rules of thumb
› Materials >65% = civils-heavy, verify sub markup carefully
› 18–22% gross on large ICP = competitive range
› Below 18% on large jobs = rarely worth the risk
